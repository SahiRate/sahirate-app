Direction
SahiRate should connect calculation + reference + local market + dealer + decision support, rather than become another calculator.
Current

Competitor benchmark
InfraLens: IS-code calculators, CPWD-DSR BOQ, IS-1200 deductions, Excel, plan/elevation/section checks.
Civimetric: description-to-estimate, IS-code quantities, CPWD/SSR pricing, regional SOR libraries, BOQ/Excel.
SiteHisab: BOQ, drawing-to-quantity AI, BBS, daily site hisab, RA bills, GST/TDS, project money.
Rebota Works: CPWD DSR 2021 + BIS consumption + CPWD productivity norms.
BuildHQ: city/quality/floor cost model, material/labour split, stage schedule, many calculators.
